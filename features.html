Administration

Administrative tools support management of books, requests, users, syllabus information, and contacts.

Why an online library system matters

A digital library portal reduces reliance on fragmented manual processes. Users can find information faster, while administrators have a central place to manage operational data.

Built around practical library workflows

  • Discover resources before visiting the library
  • Submit borrowing-related requests online
  • Access academic information in one place
  • Maintain structured user and book information
  • Support centralized administration
